## 1. Ground Truth Meets High-Level Validation

For the past 18 months, dconsult was built from the ground up without external funding or institutional datasets. As a 20-year union tradesman, Reuben Cole built this technology based on **daily, direct interaction with Detroit tradespeople, residents, and local business owners**.

In the last two weeks, two major institutional studies validated what was built from intuition and union ground truth:

1. **Mayor’s Office Disparity Report (6,000 Residents)**: Identified severe friction in municipal incentive delivery, information distribution, and equitable opportunity access.
2. **Detroit Regional Chamber & Gallup Survey (11,000 Residents)**: Revealed that **57% of Detroiters would leave if they could**, 43% face food insecurity, 39% lack job satisfaction, and 14% have easy mental health access.

> **The Core Problem**: The information exists. The incentives exist. The capital exists. **The breakdown occurs at the execution layer.** Guesswork, information opacity, and friction prevent resources from reaching the ground.

### Founder Bio — Reuben Cole

Reuben Cole is a Detroit native, 20-year union tradesman, and self-taught civic technologist. He holds a BFA in Corporate Visual Arts & Design from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (1997) and an IBM AI Fundamentals certification. After serving at Chanute Air Force Base (1995), Cole spent two decades on Detroit job sites — framing, finishing, and watching incentives fail to reach the workers who needed them most.

For 18 months, he built dconsult's entire product suite from a single laptop — coding, designing, and deploying Get Right Detroit, lots313, CBA Enterprise, and Fulcrum Financial without external funding or institutional datasets. The products were built on daily, direct interaction with Detroit tradespeople, residents, and business owners. Two weeks before this pitch, both the Mayor's 6,000-resident Disparity Report and the Gallup/Chamber 11,000-resident survey independently validated what Cole built from intuition and ground truth.

Cole's lineage — Nigerian, enslaved Virginia colonies, Cherokee — and his grandfather's legacy as the first Black graduate of West Point inform his operating model: build what others said was impossible. He is a Certified Project Manager (P1) and lives in Detroit with his family.
