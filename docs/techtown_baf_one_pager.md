## The Problem — In Detroit's Own Words

The Detroit Regional Chamber and Gallup surveyed **11,000+ Detroit-area residents** (Detroit Resident Voices Survey, 2023). The findings confirm what neighborhoods already know:

| Metric | City of Detroit | Suburbs | National Avg |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Life Evaluation: Thriving** | 40% | 52% | 51% |
| **Satisfied with Job Opportunities** | 39% | 72% | — |
| **Satisfied with Local Schools** | 30% | — | 68% |
| **Food Insecurity** | 43% | 18% | — |
| **Easy Access to Mental Health** | 14% | 30% | — |
| **Would Move if They Could** | 57% | — | — |

> **57% of Detroit residents would leave if they could.** Not because they don't love the city — because the systems meant to help them are invisible, fragmented, and hard to use.

## The Solution — dconsult Product Suite

dconsult is a Detroit-based AI consulting agency that builds **civic technology tools** turning resident data into resident action.

### Product 1: Get Right Detroit — Civic Resource Navigation Portal
Connects residents to **jobs, housing, food assistance, and community services** in under 60 seconds. Three navigation lanes: "I Need Help" → "I Am a Pro" → "I Want to Build." Directly addresses the **39% job satisfaction** and **43% food insecurity** gaps the Gallup data reveals.

### Product 2: lots313 — Neighborhood 3D Parcel Visualization
Transforms city parcel data into interactive 3D development maps. Helps community land trusts, city planners, and developers identify **housing opportunity zones** in the neighborhoods where **57% of residents want to leave**.

### Product 3: CBA Enterprise — Bank Compliance Automation
Tracks **$4B+ in Community Benefits Agreement obligations** from TD Bank, Huntington, and Comerica. Ensures CRA/CBA funds reach the neighborhoods Gallup identified as highest-need. Auditable. Scalable. $2,500/business/year.

### Product 4: Fulcrum Financial — AI-Powered Accounting Agency
Internal CPA automation handling invoicing, tax documentation, and compliance reporting for all dconsult engagements and city contracts.
